How the table works

Last one standing walks out. Everyone else meets the barrel.

  1. 01

    The table card

    Each round is Kings, Queens, or Aces. Every play claims to be that card, truth or not.

  2. 02

    Play 1 to 3

    Put cards face down. Jokers always count as the table card. Mix a fake in and the whole play is a lie.

  3. 03

    Call liar

    The next player can let it ride, or call. If you lied, you take the revolver. If you didn't, they do.

  4. 04

    Six chambers

    One live round, randomly seated. Each pull advances the cylinder. The sixth is certain.

Deck: 6 Kings · 6 Queens · 6 Aces · 2 Jokers. Five cards each.

Tip: Don't mix truth with lies. Pure honest or pure bluff. Mixing only gets you shot.
